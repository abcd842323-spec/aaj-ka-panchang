शुभ मुहूर्त Shubh Muhurat — aaj ke achhe samay

    Abhijit Muhurat dopahar ka sabse shubh samay mana jata hai. Budhvaar (Wednesday) ko Abhijit aam taur par varjit mana jata hai.

    दिन के चौघड़िया Day Choghadiya

      Choghadiya suryoday se suryast tak ke 8 बराबर hisso par aadharit hai. Amrit, Shubh aur Labh sabse achhe mane jate hain; Chal yatra ke liye theek hai.

      ⚠ ज़रूरी सूचना · Important Disclaimer

      Ye ek saral (simplified) panchang hai — poora panchang nahi. Tithi aur Nakshatra 2026–27 ke liye pehle se ganit (precomputed) table se liye gaye hain aur ye lagbhag (approximate) hain. Seema ke aas-paas wale dinon par samay me kuch minute ka, aur kabhi ek din ka, antar aa sakta hai.

      Dharmik nirnay — vrat, tyohar, vivah ya kisi bhi shubh kaam ka muhurat — ke liye apne sthaniya panchang ya pandit ji se pushti zaroor karein. Verify with your local panchang before religious decisions.

      Sunrise/Sunset NOAA solar formula se ganit hain (± kuch minute ka antar sambhav). Hijri tarikh Umm al-Qura calendar par aadharit hai — sthaniya chaand dikhne par ±1 din ka antar ho sakta hai.

      Tithi/Nakshatra table: Swiss Ephemeris + Lahiri ayanamsha se precomputed (2026–27), Purnimanta maah (Uttar Bharat paddhati). Rahu Kaal, Choghadiya aur Shubh Muhurat chune hue shahar ke suryoday–suryast se bante hain, isliye shahar badalne par ye samay badal jayenge — tithi pure Bharat ke liye ek hi rehti hai.
